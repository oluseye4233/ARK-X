## KCSE SCORING FRAMEWORK

### What is KCSE?

KCSE (Knowledge-Clarity-Specificity-Efficiency) is a proprietary scoring system measuring card effectiveness across four dimensions:

**Knowledge (K):** 0-25 points
- Domain expertise and professional depth conveyed
- Subject matter authority
- Technical or specialized knowledge

**Clarity (C):** 0-25 points
- Unambiguous communication
- Interpretability
- Freedom from confusion

**Specificity (S):** 0-25 points
- Precision and actionable detail
- Concrete guidance
- Well-defined scope

**Efficiency (E):** 0-25 points
- Token economy
- Information density
- Value per token invested

**Total KCSE Score:** Sum of all four dimensions (0-100)

### Performance Tiers

- **Exceptional:** 95-100
- **Excellent:** 90-94
- **Strong:** 85-89
- **Good:** 80-84
- **Developing:** <80
