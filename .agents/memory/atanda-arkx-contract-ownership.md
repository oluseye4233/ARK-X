---
name: ATANDA–ARK-X contract ownership
description: Durable product boundary for aligning ATANDA.ai claims and journeys with ARK-X.
---

ATANDA.ai is the presentation layer, while ARK-X is the operational system of record. A capability may be advertised as live only when an ARK-X-owned contract enforces the relevant identity, scope, role, ownership, consent and entitlement rules, persists success, and has journey evidence.

**Why:** The frontend and public site can present polished or illustrative experiences that are not proof of compatible authorization, persistence, payment, data provenance, or production behavior. Wholesale repository merging would preserve those mismatches.

**How to apply:** Reconcile complete journeys through the capability/API alignment matrix. Reuse presentation selectively, generate client contracts from ARK-X authorities, and label incomplete behavior as Beta, Preview, Demo, or Planned.
