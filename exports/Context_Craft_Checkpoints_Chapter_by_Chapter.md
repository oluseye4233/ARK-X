# How to Use This Document

This set contains **twelve checkpoint pages** plus a closing **Your Numbers Ledger** appendix. They are designed to be placed after the corresponding chapter in the final PDF layout.

Chapter titles below are **working titles** mapped to the thematic concepts the Prologue introduces. Rename them freely to match your final chapter list — the checkpoint structure does not depend on the title, only on the concept the chapter teaches.

The ramp has five stages. Each stage opens with a divider page that tells the reader what they're about to do and why this stage exists.

| Stage | Chapters | What the reader does |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Diagnose** | Ch 1–3 | Take an unflinching look. Upload, score, classify. |
| **2 · Confront** | Ch 4–5 | Sit with the result. Decide which professional they are becoming. |
| **3 · Practice** | Ch 6–8 | Build the survival skill through the game loop. Earn the first cert tier. |
| **4 · Navigate** | Ch 9–11 | Map where they could go next. Commit to a 30/90/365 discipline. |
| **5 · Prove** | Ch 12 | Compare the score they captured in Checkpoint 1 to the one they hold now. |

Within each checkpoint the six-block anatomy from the Design Guide is preserved: **Concept Recall**, **Self-Scan**, **The Platform Step**, **Your Number to Capture**, **Reflection Prompt**, **What's Coming**. One page per checkpoint. Same shape every time.

Three chapters. Three numbers. By the end of this stage the reader knows exactly where they stand in the AI economy — and they have written the proof of it in pen. Everything that comes after is a response to what they find here.

# Stage 3 · Practice

*Chapters 6 through 8 · Build the survival skill*

Three chapters and three real sessions of play. This is the stage where the book starts to *cost something* — your time. The platform's game loop (CCGE) is the engine for everything that follows; the cert tiers earned here become the multiplier on every score that comes after.

The rule for this stage: read the chapter, then play. Not the other way around. The book teaches the principle; the game teaches the reflex.
